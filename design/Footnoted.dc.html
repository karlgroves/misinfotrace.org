An open research project

Misinformation is a system.1 We're studying how it works.

Misinfo Trace detects factual narratives as they emerge, examines the evidence around them, and studies how misinformation originates and spreads — holding every claim to the same standard,2 whichever side it favors.

Explore the Project See Our Approach

1  A claim starts somewhere, moves through posts, articles and reposts, and joins a larger narrative. The problem

2  Tested, not assumed: mirrored pairs of claims run through the same method and are checked for equal treatment. Same standard

Status  Early-stage research. Parts of the method are being tested; none of this is a finished tool yet.

Same standard, regardless of side

Same claim. Same evidence. Same standard.

Our goal is to filter, classify and assess every claim by the same public-interest, classification and evidentiary rules — whichever side its political or ideological implications favor.

Position A a claim favoring one side same scope rules, classification and evidentiary standard
Position B the mirrored claim, favoring the other same scope rules, classification and evidentiary standard
Result An evidence-based assessment: supported, contradicted, disputed — or unknown.3
See how we're testing for bias

3  Unknown is a valid outcome. The method never forces a true-or-false answer the evidence can't support.
